Marcus

Paper-First Dungeon Master

Marcus has been running tabletop campaigns for over 15 years and values clarity, control, and tactile gameplay. He sees digital tools as assistants, not replacements for paper-based immersion.

Goals

  • Keep gameplay flowing without digital distractions
  • Maintain full control over stats and house rules
  • Use digital tools only when they save time

Frustrations

  • "It feels like the machine is playing the game, not me."
  • "The text is too small. I spend more time zooming than playing."
  • "I can't just edit the result."
